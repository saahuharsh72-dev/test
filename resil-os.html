Step 1 · Environment

Define the city under stress

Set the profile of the environment RESIL_OS is protecting. These values feed directly into the hazard model used by the simulation engine.

Step 2 · Constraint

Set the available budget

Every intervention has a cost. Only strategy combinations at or below this ceiling are ever treated as valid — infeasible combinations are excluded inside the engine itself, not just flagged in the UI.

Step 3 · Intervention library

Choose the interventions in play

Every selected intervention enters the strategy-generation engine. The engine will automatically build and test every affordable combination of the interventions you enable here — you never hand-pick the final strategies yourself.

— combinations possible
Step 4 · Future scenarios

Multiple futures, one set of strategies

Every feasible strategy will be tested against all three futures below, not just one. A strategy that looks strong in Mild can quietly fail in Severe — that gap is the point.

Step 5 · Uncertainty

How uncertain is each future?

Instead of one exact hazard number, each scenario is treated as a range. The engine Monte-Carlo samples inside that range so results come back as best-case / average / worst-case, not a single fragile point estimate.

Step 9 · Sensitivity analysis

Which assumptions move the needle?

One parameter is changed at a time from baseline and the model is rerun on the selected strategy. Longer bars = more influence on the final score.
